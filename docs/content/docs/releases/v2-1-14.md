## Added

- **Users & teams** replaces the separate Users and Teams pages. `/users`
  serves the merged page; `/teams` and the draft `/people` URL redirect to
  it. The left column is the team tree — All accounts / teams / Ungrouped
  for platform admins, My teams for everyone else — and the right column
  shows the account table or the selected team's members.
- **First-use access flow.** An account that can sign in but holds no team
  is kept on `/waiting-for-access` (outside the app shell, so no menu)
  until a platform admin grants a team and role. The page re-reads `/me` on
  demand, every 30 seconds, and when the window regains focus; it then
  enters the path the user originally asked for.
  - The admin side gets a dynamic **“N accounts have no team yet”** banner
    and inline **Grant** (team + role) in the Ungrouped bucket, so a fresh
    SSO account no longer lands silently without a prompt.
  - The three-step onboarding strip yields to the approval queue and to the
    no-team banner, so the banners never stack.

## Fixed

- **Platform variables can only be bound by platform admins**, through both
  the form (`var_ids`) and YAML (`variables:`). A non-admin editing a
  pipeline keeps the existing bindings but cannot add or remove one.
  Previously a team developer could bind an admin-owned variable and read
  its value from the job environment.
- **Environment metadata is admin-only.** Non-admins still receive the
  environment shell (name / slug / protection) for the trigger dropdown,
  but `secret_overrides` and `host_allowlist` are returned empty, and
  `GET /environments/{id}/secret-overrides` plus `/host-allowlist` now
  require a platform admin. This stops cross-team secret and host names
  from leaking through the global Environments surface.
- **UI actions follow the role matrix.** Variables is admin-write only;
  Secrets and Hosts actions follow the owning team's maintainer role;
  Pipelines New/Edit/Trigger/Toggle require developer and Delete requires
  maintainer; Jobs cancel/delete/re-run require developer. Team selectors
  list only teams the caller can manage; shared hosts remain admin-only.

## Upgrading

- `/teams` and `/users` both resolve to the merged Users & teams page;
  existing bookmarks keep working.
- Scripts that called `GET /environments/{id}/secret-overrides` or
  `/host-allowlist` without a platform-admin credential now receive 403.
- CI definitions that bind platform variables through YAML must be saved by
  a platform admin. Existing bindings are preserved when other users edit
  the pipeline.
